AI USAGE AND IMPACT<br><br>
Dataset focuses on understanding how people use Artificial Intelligence tools in their education, work, and daily activities. The dataset includes both students and professionals, allowing us to compare their AI usage patterns and understand how AI affects different groups of users. It contains demographic information such as age, gender, country, education level, and profession, along with information about income and the type of user

<b>Findings</b><br><br>
•	Chat GPT dominates market penetration with 411 users, followed by Google Gemini (139 users), Microsoft Copilot (104 users), Claude (97 users), and Perplexity AI (85 users).
•	Chat GPT represents the largest absolute expenditure category, while Google Gemini is situated at 27.66% of that baseline cost scale, followed closely by Microsoft Copilot at a compressed 10.9% segment level.
•	The visualization demonstrates that Student users make up 56.19% of the utilization focus, while Professional users occupy 43.81% 
•	Coding (216 users) represents the largest operational workflow application, followed sequentially by Writing (172 users), Studying (170 users), Research (125 users), Content Creation (99 users), Customer Support (87 users), and Data Analysis (86 users).
•	Chat GPT is the clear winner here, with its blue bar stretching all the way across the screen, dominating the accuracy rating.
Conclusion
This mini-project delivers an interactive Power BI analytics dashboard that evaluates the adoption trends, financial costs, and operational impacts of artificial intelligence tools across a diverse sample of user data. The data reveals a clear market concentration, with Chat GPT emerging as the primary platform of choice and software programming (Coding: 216 users) serving as the leading functional application across a balanced demographic split of 516 students and 472 professionals. By converting complex behavioral metrics into clean, high-level KPIs and data cross-filters, the system provides a production-ready blueprint for analyzing software utilization and optimizing enterprise resource investments.
